# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone who takes part in WasmCheat: issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions
and anywhere else the project is talked about in its name.

## What we ask

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not with people. Assume the other person means well and
  may not share your first language.
- **No harassment.** No insults, slurs, threats, sexual comments, personal attacks, or posting
  someone's private information. Do not keep contacting someone who asked you to stop.
- **Keep it about the work.** Stay on the bug, the change or the question in front of you. Politics,
  religion and arguments unrelated to the project belong somewhere else.
- **Help newcomers.** A question that seems basic is still a fair question. Point to the docs or
  answer it.

## Reporting a problem

If someone breaks these rules, contact the repository owner on GitHub
([@alfman99](https://github.com/alfman99)). Say what happened, where, and link to it if you can.
Reports are kept private.

For a security problem, do not open an issue: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues, pull requests and other content that breaks
these rules, and may warn, block or ban anyone from the project, for as long as they judge right.
