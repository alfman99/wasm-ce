WasmCheat is installed

Change a web app or browser game while it runs, by hand here or from your AI agent. Here is the whole of it.

  1. Open a gameA web game built with Unity, Godot or Emscripten, a YouTube Playable or an itch.io game. Let it start.
  2. Open the panelPress F12 (on a Mac ⌥ ⌘ I) and choose the WasmCheat tab at the top of DevTools. If it is not there, look under ».
  3. Scan, narrow, changeType a number you see in the game and press First Scan. Change it in the game, type the new number, press Next Scan. Click the address that is left and press Write or Freeze.

The toolbar button tells you whether the tab in front has WebAssembly. WasmCheat collects nothing and talks to no server but your own computer. Use it on games and programs you own or may test.
